# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Copperbelt University staff members and departmental teams who submit, review, approve, procure, receive, and report on institutional purchases.

## Product Purpose

The CBU Procurement System gives departments one internal workspace for purchase requisitions, approvals, supplier quotes, purchase orders, budget commitments, payments, delivery confirmation, and audit history.

## Positioning

It connects the full university procurement workflow—from a staff request to departmental approval, procurement, payment, and Stores delivery—in a single role-aware system.

## Operating Context

Users work across departments, cost centres, approval roles, suppliers, and Stores. The system supports product and service requisitions, budget controls, procurement evidence, purchase orders, and delivery tracking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Laravel and Livewire web application with role-based access.
- Roles include requester, HOD, Stores, Bursar, VC, Procurement, Auditor, and Admin.
- The public landing page is an entry point for CBU staff and departments, not a supplier-facing portal.

- Make procurement status and responsibility clear.
- Preserve accountability at every workflow stage.
